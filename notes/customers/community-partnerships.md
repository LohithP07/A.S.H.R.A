# Community Partnerships

**Owner:** Jules Park
**Budget:** $6,000 in cash and product for 2027
**Policy:** one ongoing partner per quarter, plus small donations under $150.

## Current partners
1. **Asheville Humane Society** - monthly "Paws & Pour" Saturday. 10% of drip sales, adoption tent on the patio. Raised $4,180 in 2026.
2. **River Arts District Association** - market booth, event coffee, and a gift card raffle. See [[Farmers Market Stall]].
3. **Asheville High robotics team** - coffee cart at two fundraisers; students learn the register. $1,200 in product.
4. **Hood Huggers International** - neighborhood clean-up mornings. We provide coffee and a meeting table.
5. **Literacy Council** - book drive in February; free drip for donors.

## How we choose
- The partner is local and has a clear, specific ask.
- We can show up with more than money: space, coffee, staff time, or promotion.
- The relationship does not require us to endorse a political candidate or a controversial policy.
- We can measure something: dollars raised, people served, or new customers.

## Donation tiers
- **Under $50:** gift card or coffee for an event.
- **$50-150:** product donation plus one social post.
- **$150-500:** event presence and co-branded promotion.
- **Over $500:** quarterly partnership with a written plan and a recap.

## Calendar
- February: literacy book drive.

## Guardrails
Never promise exclusivity. Never donate cash without a receipt. Post about a partner at most twice per month so the feed does not feel like a charity bulletin. Ask partners for photos and a one-line impact number for the annual recap. Invite a writer from [[Local Press Outreach]] to the Earth Day event.
