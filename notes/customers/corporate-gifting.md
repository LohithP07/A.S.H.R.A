# Corporate Gifting

**Owner:** Maya Chen with Jules
**Season:** 1 November to 18 December
**Goal:** $45,000 in gifting revenue, 38% gross margin.

## Offer
| Box | Contents | Price | Cost | Min order |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Small Thanks | 1 x 12 oz bag, sticker, note card | $32 | $18.40 | 10 |
| The Workshop | 2 x 12 oz bags, mug, note card | $58 | $33.10 | 8 |
| The Foundry | 2 x 12 oz, 1 x 2 lb, mug, chocolate, note | $92 | $51.80 | 6 |
| Custom | client-chosen bags and merch | from $110 | quoted | 25 |

Add a handwritten note for orders over 20 boxes; charge $1.50 per note for custom messages. Local delivery is free over $500; shipping is actual cost plus $6.

## Target list
- 14 downtown offices with 20-80 employees.
- 9 existing wholesale accounts, especially Juniper House and Ridgeline Grocery.
- 6 past gifting clients from 2025. Reorder rate was 44%.
- 3 real estate offices and 2 property managers.
- 2 hotels for guest welcome boxes.

## Outreach timeline
- **Oct 20:** update the one-page catalog and order form.
- **Oct 27:** email past clients with early-bird pricing (5% off before 8 November).
- **Nov 3:** Instagram post and in-store display. See [[Instagram Calendar]].
- **Nov 10:** call the top 15 prospects. Email is not enough.
- **Nov 24:** order deadline for guaranteed December 12 delivery.
- **Dec 1-15:** pack and deliver. Two staff, four-hour blocks.

## Fulfillment
Batch pack on Mondays and Thursdays. Use the holiday sleeve and a kraft box. Keep 40 extra bags and 30 mugs as buffer. Track every order in the shared sheet with a delivery date and a proof-of-delivery photo.

## Margin guardrails
Do not discount over 8%. Do not include shipping on orders under $500. If a custom request needs new print or merch, quote it separately and require 50% deposit. See [[Pricing Strategy]] and [[Packaging Design]].
