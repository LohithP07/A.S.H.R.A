# Customer Feedback Themes

**Owner:** Priya Raman
**Sources:** Google reviews, comment cards, the QR survey, DMs, and barista notes.
**Window:** July-October 2026, 214 responses.

## What people love
- **The people.** 68 mentions by name. "Nia remembered my order after one visit" is the most repeated sentence in our reviews.
- **The patio.** 41 mentions. Dog-friendly, shady in the afternoon, good for a slow hour.
- **The Foundry latte.** 37 mentions. People describe it as chocolatey without being heavy.
- **The retail bag selection.** 22 mentions; visitors like taking a local bag home.

## What needs work
1. **Wait times (32 mentions).** Saturday 9:30-11:00 is the pain point. Average 11 minutes, worst 17. Current fix: second grinder, pickup shelf, simplified morning menu. See [[Team Meeting Notes]].
2. **Seating (19).** Outlets are scarce and two tables wobble. Buy four new chairs and add two outlets near the window. Budget $900.
3. **Decaf options (14).** We run out of decaf beans once or twice a month. Keep a 5 lb backup in the freezer and mark it clearly.
4. **Price (11).** Mostly about the $7.25 seasonal drinks. The value story is not landing. Add a small sign listing ingredients and local sourcing.
5. **Music volume (8).** Afternoon playlist is too loud for laptop work. Set a 68 dB cap after 2:00 p.m.

## Quotes worth keeping
- "It feels like a living room that happens to make excellent coffee."
- "I came for the wifi and stayed for the cardamom bun."
- "Please never change the cortado."
- "The line was long, but the person at the register was kind about it."

## Response policy
Reply to every public review within 48 hours. Thank, acknowledge, and move the conversation offline. Never argue about a specific visit. Track refunds and remakes in the same sheet so we can see patterns.

## Next steps
Share the top three themes at the next team meeting. Run a two-week test of the new pickup shelf. Re-survey in January and compare. See [[Loyalty Program]] and [[Espresso Blend Development]].
