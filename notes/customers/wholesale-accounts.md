## Account health
- Visit every anchor in person once a month. Bring coffee, not a clipboard.
- Check extraction and grinder settings at each visit. Leave a recipe card.
- Track order frequency, payment timing, and complaints. A drop of 20% for two weeks triggers a call.
- Annual review in January: volume, margin, and whether the partnership still fits.

## Concentration policy
No single account should exceed 25% of wholesale revenue by the end of 2027. Add four anchors and keep the top two under 40% combined.
