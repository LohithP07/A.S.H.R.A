# Cash Flow Forecast

**Owner:** Maya Chen
**Method:** 13-week rolling forecast, updated every Monday by 10:00 a.m.

## Opening position
Week of 2 November 2026: $48,200 in operating cash, $12,000 reserved for equipment, $9,400 in the tax savings account.

## Assumptions
- Café sales: $30,000-34,000 per month, with a 12% lift in December.
- Wholesale: $14,000 per month; 60% Net 15, 40% Net 30. Collections lag 9 days on average.
- Subscription: $5,600 per month, billed on the 1st.
- Payroll: 1st and 15th, about $11,900 per cycle including taxes.
- Green coffee: two payments per month, $9,000-12,000.
- Rent: $4,850 on the 1st. Utilities: $1,300-1,800 on the 12th.
- Owner distributions: $6,000 per quarter, only if the cash floor is met.

## Guardrails
- Minimum operating cash: $25,000. Hard floor: $18,000.
- If the 13-week low dips below $30,000, delay non-critical equipment and pause owner distributions.
- If wholesale aging over 45 days exceeds $8,000, stop new credit orders until collected.

## Scenario planning
- **Base:** holiday gifting $30,000, wholesale flat, cash low $31,000 in late January.
- **Slow:** gifting $18,000 and one anchor account cuts volume 25%. Cash low $21,500. Trigger: delay the second espresso machine and reduce January roast days.
- **Strong:** gifting $45,000 and two new accounts. Cash low $39,000. Use surplus to prepay green coffee before the March price reset.

## Weekly checklist
1. Update actual bank balance and cleared checks.
2. Review receivables over 30 days with Priya.
3. Confirm payroll transfers and tax set-asides.
4. Flag any purchase over $500 before it happens.
5. Compare the 13-week low to the guardrails and note one action.
