# Pricing Strategy

**Owner:** Maya Chen
**Last full review:** August 2026
**Next review:** February 2027

## Principles
1. Price for a healthy business, not for sympathy. A 61% gross margin keeps the lights on and pays people properly.
2. Round to friendly numbers at the register, but calculate from cost, not from vibes.
3. Raise prices once a year, in small steps, with a clear story. No surprise increases mid-season.
4. Protect the everyday drip drink. It is the habit builder and the most price-sensitive item.

## Wholesale pricing
Retail bag equivalent is $19; wholesale is 30-34% below retail depending on volume. Never quote below $56 per 5 lb. Accounts over 60 lb per month can earn a 4% rebate for prompt payment, not a lower list price. Delivery is included within 8 miles; beyond that, $12 per stop.

## Cost pressure
Green coffee is 38% of bag cost. A 10% green increase adds about $0.27 per 12 oz bag. Absorb up to 5%; beyond that, adjust the menu in February. Packaging changes can offset 8-12 cents. See [[Supplier Agreements]] and [[Packaging Design]].

## 2027 plan
- February: +25 cents on espresso drinks, +50 cents on 16 oz seasonal, hold drip.
- April: wholesale list +3%, with 60 days' notice.
- September: test a $22 reserve bag with a printed producer story.
- Track price sensitivity through [[Customer Feedback Themes]] and weekly unit counts.
