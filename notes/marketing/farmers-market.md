# Farmers Market Stall

**Season:** second Saturday of April through last Saturday of October
**Location:** River Arts District Farmers Market, booth 14
**Hours:** 8:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. Setup 6:45.

## What we sell
- 12 oz retail bags: Foundry, single origin, decaf. Three for $42.
- 16 oz cold brew bottles and a small canned line.
- Drip coffee and one seasonal iced drink. No espresso; power and water are limited.
- Pastries from Hearth & Crumb, pre-boxed.
- $5 sample flights of two single origins.

## Booth setup
- 10x10 tent, two tables, navy tablecloth, chalkboard, card reader, cash box with $150.
- Banner on the back wall, bags stacked by origin, a small cupping tray for smells.
- Bring 2 gallons of water, rags, trash bags, sunscreen, and a rain cover.
- One person can run it; two are better from 9:30 to 11:30.

## Costs and break-even
Booth fee $35/week, parking $8, staffing 6 hours at $19/hour, sample and pastry cost about $85. Break-even is roughly $210 in sales. 2026 average was $486, with a low of $190 in a storm and a high of $810 on the fall festival weekend. Track weekly in [[Cash Flow Forecast]].

## Weather policy
If the market cancels, we post a story and offer preorders for Tuesday pickup. If it is raining but open, we bring the small tent and skip pastries. Never risk the card reader or the bags.

## Staffing and promotion
Rotate two staff per market; it counts as a full shift. Post the booth menu on Thursday. Offer a market-only code for [[Loyalty Program]] signups. Invite a [[Local Press Outreach]] writer to the October harvest market.
