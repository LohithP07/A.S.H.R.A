# Instagram Calendar

**Owner:** Jules Park, marketing + bar
**Cadence:** 4 feed posts, 5 stories, 1 reel per week.

## Content pillars
1. **People (30%)** - baristas, producers, regulars with permission. Faces outperform flat lays by 2.4x.
2. **Craft (25%)** - roast days, dial-in, latte art, the weird sound the roaster makes at first crack.
3. **Place (20%)** - Asheville weather, the patio, the neighborhood, local makers.
4. **Offer (15%)** - new drinks, retail bags, subscription, events. Never more than two offer posts in a row.
5. **Community (10%)** - partner spotlights, donation days, market appearances.

## Weekly rhythm
- **Monday:** week-ahead story with the limited pour-over.
- **Tuesday:** craft reel. Film vertical, 12-20 seconds, no trending audio unless it fits.
- **Thursday:** people or place photo.
- **Friday:** offer or event post with a clear call to action.
- **Saturday:** market or weekend story series.

## Caption formula
Hook (one line) -> useful detail (two lines) -> invitation (one line) -> 3-5 hashtags. Keep hashtags local: #ashevillecoffee #riverartsdistrict #avlfood #smallbatchcoffee. No hashtag walls.

## Metrics
Track saves, shares, profile visits, and code redemptions. Follower count is a vanity metric; saved posts and DM questions are the signal. Review monthly with [[Loyalty Program]] data. Anything under 1.5% engagement for three weeks gets retired.
