## Pitch angles
1. **The 12-kilo rule.** Why we refuse to grow past what one roaster can do well.
2. **The $6 coffee.** A transparent breakdown of where a latte dollar goes. This one needs [[Pricing Strategy]] numbers.
3. **Hiring people who have never made coffee.** Our paid training model and why it works. See [[Hiring a Barista]].
4. **A neighborhood supply chain.** Milk from 38 miles away, bread from two blocks away.

## Press kit
- 6 high-res photos: roaster portrait, café exterior, pour-over, team, bags, patio.
- 120-word boilerplate and 40-word version.
- Founder bios and headshots.
- Three customer quotes with permission.
- Fact sheet: founded 2021, 11 employees, 612 reviews, 4.8 stars.

## Outreach rules
Personal email, no attachments over 2 MB, one follow-up after seven days, then stop. Never send a mass BCC. Offer a visit and a tasting, not a "press release." If a story runs, thank the writer and share it once. See [[Brand Positioning]] and [[Community Partnerships]].
