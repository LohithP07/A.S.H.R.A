# Loyalty Program

**Working name:** The Regulars Club
**Owner:** Jules Park
**Target launch:** 1 February 2027

## Why now
Our best customers already come 3-5 times a week, but we have no way to thank them beyond remembering their order. A lightweight digital punch card should lift visit frequency 8-12% and give us an email list we actually own. See [[Customer Feedback Themes]].

## Mechanics
- Join by email or phone at the register. No app, no plastic card.
- Earn 1 star per $1 spent. 90 stars = a free drink or 12 oz bag.
- Birthday month: double stars and a free pastry.
- **Forge tier** at 400 stars per year: free brew bar upgrade, early access to limited lots, one free class.
- Points expire after 12 months of inactivity. Keep the policy visible.

## Economics
Assume 1,100 members in year one, 35% active monthly, average ticket $8.40. At a 6% effective reward cost, the program runs about $1,900 per month in product and platform fees. It needs to drive roughly $4,200 in incremental margin to break even. Model in [[Monthly P&L Review]].

## Launch plan
1. Choose a platform that works with Square and supports SMS. Budget $79/month.
2. Train every barista on the pitch: "Want stars for this? It takes ten seconds."
3. Soft launch to 50 regulars for two weeks; fix friction before public launch.
4. Launch with a week of double stars and a window sign.
5. Measure signup rate, active rate, and redeemed rewards at 30/60/90 days.

## Guardrails
- Never sell or share member data.
- Do not make non-members feel second-class. The free water and warm welcome stay free.
- Cap the program at one free drink per member per visit.
