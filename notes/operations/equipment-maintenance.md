# Equipment Maintenance

**Owner:** Theo Alvarez
**Service log:** clipboard by the roaster, plus photos in the shared drive.

| Machine | Age | Service interval | Vendor |
|---|---|---|---|

| La Marzocco Linea PB | 4 years | 6 months | Asheville Espresso Fix |

| Bunn batch brewer | 2 years | descale quarterly | in-house |
| True prep fridge | 6 years | coils monthly | Cool Ridge HVAC |

## Daily
- Purge steam wand after every milk pitcher. Wipe with a damp cloth, not a wet rag.
- Backflush the groups with water at close. Detergent backflush every Sunday.
- Empty the roaster chaff tray after the second batch. Never leave chaff overnight.
- Check fridge temps at open and close. Log anything above 41 F.

## Weekly
- Vacuum the roaster cooling tray and check the flame sensor.
- Clean grinder hoppers and weigh a 20 g test dose for drift.
- Run the dishwasher cleaning cycle and clean the spray arms.
- Inspect cords, gaskets, and the espresso drain.

## Monthly / quarterly
- Descale the batch brewer with citric solution; flush twice.
- Vacuum condenser coils on the prep fridge.
- Change the roaster's thermal probe gasket at every 250 hours.
- Have the espresso machine inspected before the holiday rush. Book the September slot in July.

## If something breaks
1. Stop using the equipment and tag it "out of service."
2. Text Theo with a photo and the serial number.
3. Use the backup grinder or batch brewer. Do not improvise repairs.
4. Log the downtime and any lost sales for the [[Monthly P&L Review]].
