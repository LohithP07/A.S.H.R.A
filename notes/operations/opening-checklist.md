# Café Opening Checklist

**Shift:** opener arrives 6:15 a.m. Doors at 7:00 a.m.
**Owner:** shift lead on duty. Initial each line in the paper log.

## Before the bar
- [ ] Disarm alarm, turn on entry lights, check the back door is locked.
- [ ] Walk the café: no water on the floor, no damaged furniture, patio gate secure.
- [ ] Empty the tip jar into the safe bag. Two people count on Mondays.
- [ ] Check the pastry delivery against the invoice. Refrigerate immediately.
- [ ] Pull the previous night's cold brew and check the date label.

## Espresso and brew
- [ ] Turn on the La Marzocco at 6:20. Confirm steam pressure reaches 1.3 bar.
- [ ] Purge both groups for 5 seconds. Wipe and brush the shower screens.
- [ ] Dial in the Foundry blend: 18.5 g in, 38 g out, 27-29 seconds. Taste it. Adjust only if sour or bitter.
- [ ] Check the batch brewer temperature and brew a 12 oz test batch.
- [ ] Confirm oat, whole, and skim milk are dated and below 41 F.

## Front of house
- [ ] Wipe tables, condiment station, and door glass. Sweep the entry.
- [ ] Fill napkins, lids, cups, and retail bags. Face the retail shelf.
- [ ] Write the day's limited pour-over on the chalkboard.
- [ ] Set the sidewalk sign and unlock the patio gate at 6:50.
- [ ] Count the register till to $200. Record the count.

## Handoff notes
Write anything unusual in the shift log: equipment noises, a broken item, an unhappy guest, a delivery short. If the grinder stalls, stop and call Theo. Do not adjust the burrs yourself. See [[Equipment Maintenance]] and [[Training Manual]].
