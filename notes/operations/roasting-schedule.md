# Roasting Schedule

**Roaster:** Theo Alvarez
**Roaster:** Diedrich IR-12, 12 kg nominal, 9 kg comfortable batch
**Current cadence:** Monday and Thursday, 7:00 a.m. to 2:00 p.m.

## Weekly plan
| Day | Batch focus | Volume |
|---|---|---|
| Monday | Foundry espresso, decaf, café drip | 5 batches / 45 kg |
| Thursday | Single origins, wholesale, subscription | 6 batches / 54 kg |
| Friday | Emergency top-up only | max 2 batches |

## Rest and freshness
- Espresso rests 5-7 days before service; filter rests 3-5 days.
- Bags leave the roastery no earlier than 48 hours after roast.
- Wholesale orders are roasted Tuesday for Wednesday delivery and Friday for Saturday delivery.

## Monday run sheet
1. 7:00 warm-up and green inventory check.
2. 7:30 Foundry batch 1. Log charge temp, first crack, drop temp, weight loss.
3. 8:15 Foundry batch 2. Taste against the reference sample.
4. 9:15 decaf. Clean the chaff tray after.
5. 10:30 café drip blend.
6. 12:00 cupping table for Thursday's lots. Invite one staff member.

## Green coffee
Keep 3-4 weeks of Foundry components on hand. Colombia Huila is the backbone; if it is late, substitute Nariño and note the change. Never blend a new origin into the core without a cupping and a two-week trial. See [[Supplier Agreements]] and [[Espresso Blend Development]].

## Quality gates
- First crack should land between 9:15 and 10:15 for the core blend.
- Weight loss target: 14.5-16.0%.
- Any batch outside range is held, cupped, and marked "trial" before sale.

## Wholesale cutoff
Order forms close Sunday at 5:00 p.m. Late orders roll to the next roast. See [[Wholesale Fulfillment]].
