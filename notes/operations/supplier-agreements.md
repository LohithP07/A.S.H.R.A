## Packaging and supplies
- **Carolina Paper Co.:** bags, labels, cups. 10-day lead; free freight over $600.
- **Mountain Janitorial:** cleaning supplies, monthly. No auto-renewal.

## Terms and guardrails
- No exclusivity without a volume commitment of at least 200 lb per month.
- All food suppliers must provide a current liability certificate and allergen sheet.
- Keep two approved backups for every critical input. Milk backup: Hickory Dale Dairy. Bag backup: Printworks AVL.
- Any price increase over 8% requires a written notice and a margin review. See [[Pricing Strategy]].
