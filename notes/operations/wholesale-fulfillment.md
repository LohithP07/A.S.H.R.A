# Wholesale Fulfillment

**Owner:** Priya Raman, wholesale lead
**Backup:** Maya Chen

## Order cycle
- **Sunday 5:00 p.m.** Order form closes. Priya exports the sheet into the pack list.
- **Monday** Confirm weights and invoice amounts by email. Flag any account over its credit limit.
- **Tuesday / Friday** Roast, rest, grind if requested, and pack.
- **Wednesday / Saturday** Deliver before 10:00 a.m. Local accounts can pick up after 11:00.

## Packing standard
- Whole bean by default. Grind only on a written request, and label the grind date.
- 5 lb bags for restaurants, 12 oz retail bags for shelves.
- Every box includes a roast-date sticker, recipe card, and a handwritten note for accounts over 20 lb.
- Use the reusable crate system for accounts within 5 miles. Crates come back on the next delivery.

## Delivery route
1. West Asheville: 7:30-8:30 (four stops)
2. Downtown: 8:45-9:30 (three stops)
3. River Arts: 9:45-10:15 (two stops)

Route order changes on market Saturdays. See [[Farmers Market Stall]].

## Invoicing and terms
Net 15 for accounts under 20 lb per week; Net 30 for anchors with a signed agreement. Late fee 1.5% per month after 30 days. Priya reviews aging every Friday and sends a friendly nudge at day 21.

## Service recovery
If an account gets a bad batch, replace it same week, no questions. Log the lot number and the complaint. Three complaints from one account triggers a cupping with Theo and a written plan.
