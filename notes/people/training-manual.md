# Staff Training Manual

**Owner:** Priya Raman
**Version:** 4.2, September 2026
**Sign-off required:** espresso, register, allergens, opening, closing

## Week 1: Orientation
- Tour the café, roastery, storage, and safety equipment.
- Review the employee handbook, pay schedule, break policy, and shift-swap rules.
- Shadow two opening shifts and two closing shifts.
- Learn the menu names and the difference between a latte, flat white, cappuccino, and cortado.
- Make and taste drip coffee, cold brew, and one espresso. Learn to describe sweetness, acidity, and body without jargon.

## Week 2: Bar basics
- Espresso recipe: 18.5 g in, 38 g out, 27-29 seconds. Weigh every shot during training.
- Milk: whole to 135-140 F for a 12 oz latte. Oat milk stretches faster; stop earlier.
- Steam wand cleaning after every pitcher. Purge before every use.
- Register: modifiers, gift cards, refunds, and the loyalty signup. Never process a refund over $20 alone.
- Allergens: oat, almond, soy, dairy, gluten, egg, sesame. Use a clean pitcher for dairy allergies and change gloves.

## Week 3: Guided service
- Two hours on register with a trainer nearby.
- Make every fifth drink solo, then every third, then a full 30-minute bar block.
- Practice three recovery scripts: wrong drink, long wait, spilled drink.
- Learn opening and closing checklists. See [[Opening Checklist]].

## Week 4: Sign-off
- Make five drinks in a row to standard while narrating the steps.
- Pass a 15-question menu and allergen quiz (80% minimum).
- Complete one opening and one closing with a trainer observing.
- Receive written feedback and a 30-day goals sheet.

## Ongoing
- Monthly 30-minute coffee class: origins, processing, roasting, tasting.
- Quarterly latte art practice, paid, on a slow Tuesday.
- Annual food safety refresher.
- Anyone can propose a menu idea; bring a recipe and a cost estimate.

## Standards we do not compromise
Fresh towels, clean steam wands, dated milk, correct shot weights, and a genuine greeting. Speed comes after consistency. See [[Equipment Maintenance]] and [[Hiring a Barista]].
