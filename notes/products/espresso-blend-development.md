# Espresso Blend Development

**Blend name:** Foundry
**Owner:** Theo Alvarez
**Current version:** 3.1 (July 2026)

## Components
| Coffee | Share | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Colombia Huila, washed | 60% | Chocolate base, red apple, body |
| Guatemala Antigua, washed | 30% | Caramel sweetness, round acidity |
| Ethiopia Guji, natural | 10% | Blueberry lift, aroma |

## Target profile
Milk chocolate, cooked plum, brown sugar, a finish that stays sweet as the cup cools. Low bitterness, medium body. It should taste good as a straight shot but shine in a 12 oz latte. Not smoky, not aggressively citrusy.

## Roast plan
- Charge 200 C, 9 kg batch.
- First crack 9:45-10:15. Development 18-21% of total time.
- Drop 211-214 C. Weight loss 14.8-15.6%.
- Rest 5-7 days before dialing in. Peak flavor days 8-18.

## Dial-in standard
18.5 g in, 38 g out, 27-29 seconds, 93 C water, 9 bar. If sour: grind finer or raise temp 0.5 C. If bitter: grind coarser or lower temp. Change one variable at a time and taste again.

## Current test log
- v3.0 was too bright as a straight shot; the Guji share dropped from 15% to 10%.
- v3.1 scored 8.1/10 in staff cupping. Highest marks for sweetness and finish.
- Next trial: 5% Brazil Cerrado to add nuttiness and reduce cost by $0.14 per bag. Cup on 4 November.

## Retail and wholesale
12 oz bags at $19; 5 lb wholesale at $62. Keep a printed recipe card for accounts and offer a dial-in visit after delivery. See [[Pricing Strategy]].

## Quality risks
- Colombia harvest delays could force a Nariño substitution. Do not change the blend without a two-week trial and a note to wholesale accounts.
- Natural Guji lots vary; taste every new lot before committing.
