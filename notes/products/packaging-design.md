# Packaging Design

**Owner:** Jules Park with Maya
**Status:** final artwork due 15 December for a February print run.

## Formats
- 12 oz retail bag, matte kraft with a deep green label.
- 2 lb retail bag for regulars and gifting.
- 5 lb wholesale bag, unprinted kraft with a color-coded sticker.
- Holiday gift box, 10x7x4 inches, with a sleeve and a note card. See [[Corporate Gifting]].

## Design direction
A field-guide feel: botanical line drawings, generous white space, and a single accent color per origin. The Fern & Forge wordmark stays small and confident at the top. Roast date stamped by hand, origin and tasting notes printed. No fake burlap texture, no "artisan" script.

## Color system
- **Foundry:** deep forest green (#1F3D2B) with warm cream.
- **Colombia:** terracotta (#B4552D).
- **Guatemala:** ochre (#C58B2A).
- **Ethiopia:** plum (#5A2A3B).
- **Decaf:** slate blue (#2E4057).

## Sustainability
- Bags: 100% recyclable kraft with a tin tie, no foil lining for whole bean.
- Labels: FSC-certified paper, water-based adhesive.
- Wholesale crates: reusable, deposit $12, tracked by account.
- No plastic windows. Compostable mailers for online orders.

## Copy rules
Origin, producer, process, altitude, tasting notes, roast date, and a 40-word story. No vague words like "smooth" without a concrete note. See [[Brand Positioning]].
