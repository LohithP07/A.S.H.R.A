## Prep and shelf life
- Maple-miso syrup: 1 part white miso, 3 parts maple, 1 part water. Simmer, cool, keep 14 days.
- Apple butter: buy local, keep refrigerated, 21 days after opening.
- Cranberry syrup: 10 days refrigerated. Shake before use.
- Label every squeeze bottle with date and initials.
