# 2026 Business Plan

**Owner:** Maya Chen, co-founder
**Last updated:** 14 October 2026
**Planning horizon:** January to December 2027

## North star
Fern & Forge should be the coffee company Asheville reaches for when it wants something that tastes like this city: warm, a little wild, and made by people who care. In 2027 we grow revenue by 18% without opening a second café, and we protect the 30-hour workweek our team actually likes.

## Where we ended 2026
- Revenue: $612,400 (café $398,000, wholesale $149,000, subscriptions and gifting $65,400).
- Gross margin: 61.2%; labor at 29.8% of sales.
- 11 employees, 7 full time. Two people crossed their three-year anniversary.
- Wholesale grew 34%, but two accounts make up 46% of that channel.
- Weekend wait times averaged 11 minutes at peak, our most common complaint.

## 2027 priorities
1. **Throughput without burnout.** Second espresso machine for the weekend bar and a simplified morning menu. See [[Opening Checklist]] and [[Equipment Maintenance]].
2. **Wholesale concentration risk.** Add four anchor accounts and move one large account onto a 12-month agreement. See [[Wholesale Accounts]].
3. **Subscription reset.** Relaunch the Roaster's Circle with better retention and a clearer value story. The old program quietly churned 42% last year. See [[Loyalty Program]].
4. **Margin discipline.** Hold green coffee cost increases to 6% through forward contracts and recipe updates. See [[Pricing Strategy]].
5. **Neighborhood presence.** Six community events, two press features, and a monthly farmers market from April through October. See [[Community Partnerships]].

## Quarterly milestones
- **Q1:** install second grinder, finish 2026 audit, relaunch subscription waitlist.
- **Q2:** open wholesale pipeline of 12 qualified leads, publish new packaging, hire one weekend barista.
- **Q3:** peak-season staffing plan, subscription at 180 active members, 90-day cash floor of $30,000.
- **Q4:** holiday gifting at $45,000, annual P&L close, decide on second location research.

## Risks
- Green coffee volatility and shipping delays could add 4-7 points to COGS.
- A second café would stretch management thin; we are not committing in 2027.
- Key-person risk in roasting. Theo must train a backup roaster by June.

- Choose between a 2-group and 3-group machine. Facilities can support either.
- Confirm whether to keep the current 7:00 a.m. opening on Sundays.
