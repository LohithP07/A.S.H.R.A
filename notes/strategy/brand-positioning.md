# Brand Positioning

## The one-liner
**Coffee with a sense of place.** Fern & Forge roasts small batches in the back of our Lexington Avenue café, using beans from six producer partners and a roast style that favors sweetness over smoke.

## Who we are for
- **The daily regular:** 28-45, walks or bikes to the café, cares about quality but not fussiness, spends $6-9 per visit.
- **The curious visitor:** in town for a weekend, looking for a local taste, likely to buy a bag to take home.
- **The careful wholesale partner:** a restaurant or shop that wants a reliable house espresso and a roaster who answers the phone.

## What makes us different
1. **Roasted here, every week.** We never sell coffee older than 21 days. Every bag has a roast date, not a best-by date.
2. **A comfort-first flavor profile.** Chocolate, cooked fruit, brown sugar. We save the bright, floral experiments for the monthly limited release.
3. **Real hospitality.** We learn names, we remember orders, and we do not make anyone feel silly for ordering a vanilla latte.
4. **Neighborhood economics.** We buy milk from a dairy 38 miles away, bread from a bakery two blocks over, and pay above the state average for baristas.

## Voice
Warm, plainspoken, a little playful. Short sentences. No jargon on the menu. We say "coffee" more than "extraction." We never punch down at other shops or at people who like dark roast.

## Proof points
- 4.8 stars across 612 Google reviews.
- 71% of café revenue comes from returning customers.
- Two Golden Fork nominations for Best Coffee in Buncombe County.
- Wholesale partners stay an average of 26 months.

## What we are not
We are not a third-wave temple, a coworking lounge, or a subscription-first brand. We do not need to be the cheapest, the trendiest, or the biggest. We need to be the one people miss when they are away.
