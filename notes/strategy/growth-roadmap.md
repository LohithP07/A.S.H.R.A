# Growth Roadmap

A three-horizon plan for the next 18 months. This is the working map; the [[2026 Business Plan]] holds the annual targets.

## Horizon 1: Make the current shop hum (0-6 months)
- Cut the morning menu from 19 items to 13. Keep the top sellers, retire the three slowest sandwiches.
- Add a second espresso grinder and a dedicated pickup shelf. Target: median drink time under 4 minutes at 9:00 a.m.
- Rewrite the opening routine so one person can open safely. See [[Opening Checklist]].
- Move wholesale ordering to a simple shared form. Target: zero missed weekly orders.

**Success signals:** wait-time complaints down 50%, labor cost flat, café revenue up 6%.

## Horizon 2: Deepen wholesale (3-12 months)
- Build a list of 30 target restaurants, bakeries, and boutiques within 20 miles.
- Package a "house program" with training, recipe cards, and a monthly check-in. See [[Wholesale Fulfillment]].
- Add a second roast day so wholesale orders ship within 48 hours. See [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Target: 4 new anchor accounts, 18% wholesale growth, no single account above 25% of channel revenue.

## Horizon 3: Recurring revenue (9-18 months)
- Relaunch the subscription with 3 bag sizes and a choose-your-own cadence.
- Test a $38 gift box for the holidays. See [[Corporate Gifting]].
- Explore a small event space in the roastery bay on weekday evenings. Research only; no build-out yet.

## Capacity guardrails
The Diedrich IR-12 can comfortably produce 260 lb per week on two roast days. Above 320 lb we need a third day or a larger machine. The café bar tops out around 95 transactions per hour with two baristas. We will not commit to growth that breaks either constraint. See [[Equipment Maintenance]].

## Kill criteria
Pause the second-location study if cash falls below $25,000, if key-person turnover hits two people in a quarter, or if wholesale margin drops below 48%.
